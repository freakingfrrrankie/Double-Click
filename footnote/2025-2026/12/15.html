Ida Craddock, Heavenly Bridegrooms: An Unintentional Contribution to the Erotogenetic Interpretation of Religion (privately printed, 1894). Konior, 'Angelsexual' (see 13), revisits Craddock's writing as a late-19th-century forerunner in the history of erotic relations with non-human entities.
