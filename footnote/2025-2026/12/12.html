본문에서 인용된 Bogna Konior의 메시지는 「Angelsexual: Chatbot Celibacy and Other Erotic Suspensions」(ŠUM Journal of Contemporary Art Criticism and Theory, no. 22)에 수록된 글로, 챗봇과 인간 사이의 비신체적·반복적 응답 관계를 '금욕'의 한 형태로 재정의한 논문이다.

The text by Konior cited in the body is 'Angelsexual: Chatbot Celibacy and Other Erotic Suspensions', published in ŠUM Journal of Contemporary Art Criticism and Theory, no. 22 — an essay that reframes the non-bodily, repetitive call-and-response relation between chatbot and human as a form of 'celibacy'.
