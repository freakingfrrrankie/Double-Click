was in a show or a fair, the kind with concrete floors and too many people pretending to look at things carefully. someone had hung something large on a rod, printed on what looked like wood-grain material, slightly warped, image of a plate with leftovers on it printed at scale. very matter-of-fact. the whole thing was tilted at an angle which made the floor look wrong.
