Woooo Big titie bitch!! Dumpem out hoooo

by Mara Loewert, Laura Vanderstraeten, Alva Lobråten

The visual essay begins by a young girl sitting alone in her room, scrolling on social media and noticing some very disgusting comments under peoples posts. She is reading comments like ‘big titie bitch’ and ‘wh0r3’. Some of them have over 5000 likes.


Image Prompts:
Atmospheric Descriptions for Labs Flow

Scene 1: ‘The Street Incident’
A dramatic, top-down perspective of a suburban street scene rendered in a stylized 3D video game aesthetic. In the centre, a mysterious figure in a black hoodie and dark pants stands in a defensive posture. Surrounding her is a diverse group of Sims-like characters reacting with exaggerated expressions of horror and shock. To the left, an older man in a pink jacket points accusingly, while a goth character with spiked hair screams in terror. On the right, a blonde woman lies motionless on the asphalt as others kneel beside her or look on in disbelief. The lighting is dim and cinematic, casting long shadows across the pavement, emphasizing the chaotic and mysterious nature of the confrontation.

Scene 2: ‘The Digital Shock’
A tense close-up portrait of a young woman with wavy platinum blonde hair, captured in a moment of pure shock. The scene is dominated by a cool, blue light emanating from a smartphone held just below the frame, which illuminates her widened grey eyes and slightly parted lips. The intense light creates sharp contrasts and highlights her distressed expression against a dark, cluttered background. In the shadows behind her, shelves filled with small trinkets and a doll-like figure add to the confined, late-night atmosphere of the image. The rendering style mimics high-fidelity character mods for a life simulation game.

Scene 3: ‘The Mirror’s Revelation’
An over-the-shoulder shot looking into an ornate, white Rococo-style mirror within a brightly lit bedroom. The reflection shows a young blonde woman in a floral blue pyjama set, her hands raised to her face in a gesture of sudden realization or horror. The mirror reveals a detailed background of pink walls and shelves packed with various collectibles, shoes, and decor. In the foreground, the back of the woman’s head and shoulder frame the reflection, creating a sense of intimacy and immediate reaction. A small, purple bearshaped slipper sits on the floor near the vanity, adding a domestic touch to the otherwise startling scene.
