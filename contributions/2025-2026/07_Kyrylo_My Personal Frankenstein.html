My Personal Frankenstein

by Kyrylo Nechtailo

Since summer 2025, I had been exploring more and more videos on YouTube about creating OFM (OnlyFans models). Every couple of days, I would discover a new OFM account on Instagram. The whole idea of sculpting something for the public — something that could also be monetised — had been sitting in the back of my mind for a long time. In February, I finally brought those thoughts forward and began my creation.

26 February 2026

I have just started the process. I am using ComfyUI and, for now, I am testing the model on my own face. I took a selfie with Photo Booth on my Mac and created a full-body reconstruction from all sides against a white background. For now, I am fascinated by the quality and precision of the recreation of my body, even though the software only had images from my head to my neck.

27 February 2026

Kristi and Frankie mentioned a film about a girl who cut pieces from her exes and used them to create the perfect partner. What material should I use for my model?

13 March 2026

I visited the exhibitions Bellezza e Bruttezza and Picture Perfect at Bozar. I think the ideas from these exhibitions could help me develop the subject of my design.
From the older paintings, I liked the way Titian enhanced beauty in his portraits. In the modern section of the exhibition there was an AI artwork, Photomaton, by François Bellabas, consisting of a monitor, a camera and six buttons. Each button triggered a specific prompt and altered your appearance, making you look either more feminine or more masculine.
So I am thinking: if I have this AI machine, and it can also generate a full body from a single selfie, how could I ‘improve’ each person to become attractive to an OF audience?

14 March 2026

I just remembered there was a section about the obsession of royal families and painters with unconventional beauty: dwarfs, jesters and people covered entirely in hair — like Petrus Gonsalvus. What if we also gave more attention to unconventional beauty on Instagram and OF? What actually creates attraction — humour, beauty or something else entirely?

2 April 2026

I finally understand more clearly what I want to make. I found a reel about Google Flow Lab, which offers free unlimited Nano Banana generation, so I started creating images with elongated chins and subtle fine-tuning of ears.

3 April 2026

I now have final full-body images from all sides. From this point onwards, I can start creating scenes for this person.

22 April 2026

Today I tried to create an OF account, but apparently it is prohibited to upload AI-generated content. I looked for alternatives and settled on Fanvue and Reddit. However, I could not pass verification on Fanvue, so I stayed with Reddit.

23 April 2026

I just finished the fictional biography, created the subreddit r/boxandscars and uploaded the first post:
